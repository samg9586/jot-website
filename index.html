quiet includes your data

privacy isn't fine print. it's the design.

"i'm not comfortable sending my notes to an ai" is the right instinct. jot is built around it.

audio

never stored

your voice becomes text on-device and the recording is discarded immediately. there is no audio file to leak, subpoena, or train on — it doesn't exist.

notes

local-first

notes live on your device. nothing transits unless you ask for intelligence on a specific note — and you can see exactly when that happens.

server

ephemeral by design

when a note is processed, the server sees it only long enough to do the work — then the content is deleted. results come back to your device, not a cloud archive.

journal

the sensitive class stays home

reflections and journal entries never auto-process and never leave the device unless you explicitly say so. some notes are just yours.
